About Me

Hi, I’m Cynthia Ndhlovu —Co- founder of Noble Juice and Noble Stationery, based in the heart of Pretoria, South Africa. With over 6 years of experience in the banking sector, and qualifications including an NQF Level 5 in Banking and a Diploma in Accounting and Financial Computing, I bring a strong foundation in finance, customer service, and operational excellence to every business I build. These skills have helped me manage costs, streamline processes, and scale my ventures with purpose.My entrepreneurial journey began with a single homemade blend, which has now grown into Noble Juice — a dairy beverage brand that proudly supplies juice in bulk to funeral parlors, caterers, and events. Alongside that, I run Noble Stationery, delivering quality stationery and office supplies with a personal touch. I’m currently studying Computer Practice N5 and Communications Management N5 to further sharpen my business and tech skills. When I’m not managing orders or studying, you’ll likely find me enjoying one of my favorite pastimes — fishing. It’s where I find calm, clarity, and inspiration for what’s next
